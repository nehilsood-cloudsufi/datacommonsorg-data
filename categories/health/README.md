# Health Category

Health statistics, disease prevalence, insurance coverage, medical data, and public health indicators.

## Datasets

| Dataset | Description | Country | Geographic Levels |
|---------|-------------|---------|-------------------|
| sahie | US Census Small Area Health Insurance Estimates | USA | state, county |
| nchs_brfss_asthma | CDC BRFSS/NCHS Asthma Prevalence | USA | country, state |
| nyu_diabetes_tennessee | NYU Diabetes Data for Tennessee | USA | state, county |
| social_vulnerability_index | CDC Social Vulnerability Index | USA | county |
| IndiaNSS_HealthAilments | India National Sample Survey - Health Ailments | India | country, state |
| southkorea_health | South Korea Health Statistics | South Korea | country, region |
| mongolia_health | Mongolia Health Statistics | Mongolia | country, region |
